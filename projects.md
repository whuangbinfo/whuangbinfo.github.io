# Cancer-associated Fibroblast (CAF) Heterogeneity
## 1. Emerging Inflammatory CAF (iCAF) upon neoadjuvant chemotherapy (NAC)
By integrating multiple public single-cell RNAseq datasets from muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC) patients that received NAC, I aim to characterize the major subclusters of CAFs that emerge and could potentially contribute to NAC resistance. In brief, I identified 4 major classes: iCAF, myofibroblastic fibroblasts (myCAF), Hybrid i/myCAF and double-negative CAF (DNCAF) which can be easily sorted out using flow cytometry with two generic markers: &alpha;SMA and IL6.
