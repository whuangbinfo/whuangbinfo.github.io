### Postdoctoral Research — Houston Methodist Research Institute
- Identified **NHE6** as a therapeutic target in multiple myeloma.  
- Discovered **RARγ/IFN-β** pathways driving carfilzomib resistance.  
- Supported CPRIT grant *“Targeting NHE6 to improve clinical efficacy of daratumumab in myeloma” (RP220639).*  

**NHE6-driven endosome-autophagy axis confers proteasome inhibitor resistance in multiple myeloma**  
Chen L\*, Cheng F\*, **Huang Y-H\***, *et al.* [*(Drug Resistance Updates 2026)*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drup.2026.101436) — *\*co-first authors*

- Integrated public MM transcriptomic cohorts (CoMMpass, GEO) to identify **NHE6 (*SLC9A6*)** as upregulated in proteasome inhibitor (PI) non-responders.  
- High NHE6 expression is associated with shorter progression-free and overall survival.  
- NHE6 acidifies the endosomal lumen, driving early-to-late endosome maturation and autophagic flux that blunts PI-induced apoptosis.  
- Genetic (CRISPR) or pharmacologic (EMD-87580) NHE6 inhibition re-sensitized MM cells to **bortezomib** and **carfilzomib** in vitro and in PI-resistant xenograft models.
